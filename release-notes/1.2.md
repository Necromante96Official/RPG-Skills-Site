[h1]🌟 Update 1.2 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Gourmet Master, Alchemy Master and Ranch Master, max level 150, Leadership Aura and longer-range quick stack to chests.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Gourmet Master:[/b] new 5-level class.
[*] [b]Gourmet Master:[/b] increases food buff duration by 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 300%.
[*] [b]Gourmet Master:[/b] at level 5, Always Full Belly makes you eat automatically when hunger drops below 95%.
[*] [b]Alchemy Master:[/b] new 5-level class.
[*] [b]Alchemy Master:[/b] increases potion buff and Stink Flask duration by 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 300%.
[*] [b]Alchemy Master:[/b] at level 5, Auto Potion uses health potions automatically when health gets low.
[*] [b]Ranch Master:[/b] new 5-level class.
[*] [b]Ranch Master:[/b] speeds up taming, breeding and baby growth.
[*] [b]Ranch Master:[/b] at level 5, ranch animals killed in the husbandry zone drop 5x more items.
[*] [b]Leadership Aura:[/b] new Realm Master passive at level 5.
[*] [b]Leadership Aura:[/b] colonists get bonuses to damage, health, damage reduction and movement speed.
[*] [b]Max level:[/b] raised to 150 to fit the new classes.
[*] [b]Settings:[/b] the quick stack to chests range can be adjusted.
[/list]

[h2]🛠️ Quality of life[/h2]
[list]
[*] Quick stacking inventory items to nearby chests and barrels reaches farther.
[/list]

[h2]⚡ Performance[/h2]
[list]
[*] Less repeated background work, avoiding freezes and heavy FPS drops.
[*] Ranch Master's ranch animals weigh less on the game, avoiding small freezes in worlds with many mobs.
[/list]

[h2]🤝 Compatibility with Safe Haven QOL[/h2]
[list]
[*] With Safe Haven QOL installed alongside, RPG Skills helps create and apply its settings.
[*] With Safe Haven QOL 0.9, RPG Skills fills in new settings options without erasing choices already made.
[/list]

[h2]🔧 Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Wealth Master's Gold Strike gives gold when hitting enemies from the first class level.
[*] Using mounts no longer freezes the game while character bonuses are updated.
[*] The status screen no longer freezes when receiving server data during gameplay.
[/list]

[h2]💰 Wealth Master[/h2]
[list]
[*] Gold Strike's gold per hit grows with class levels, up to 50 gold per hit at level 5.
[/list]
