[h1]🌟 Update 4.3 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

A new Classes tab, with everything each level gives in plain sight, and Necromancy allies that no longer create enemies against you.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Classes tab:[/b] new grimoire-style tab.
[*] [b]Classes tab:[/b] classes are split by category: Combat, Survival, Movement, Utility and Necromancy.
[*] [b]Classes tab:[/b] each category shows how many points you have spent in it.
[*] [b]Classes tab:[/b] every class shows up as a card with its icon, name and the points already placed.
[*] [b]Bonus per level:[/b] when you pick a class, the side panel shows the bonus of every level at once, from 1 to 5.
[*] [b]Bonus per level:[/b] the panel also shows what each level unlocks.
[*] [b]Bonus per level:[/b] levels you already have are gold, and the next one is highlighted.
[/list]

[h2]🔧 Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*] An Evil's Protector summoned by the ritual no longer opens portals that release enemies against you.
[*] Other allies that launch creatures or leave something behind, like the toy soldiers and the trenchcoat goblins, also no longer bring enemies into the fight.
[*] An allied Phoenix no longer leaves an egg when it dies.
[*] Your allies keep fighting with all their usual attacks.
[*] Enemies that you or your allies defeat still behave as always.
[/list]
