[*] [b]Progression config:[/b] sets the level curve, max level, points per class and reset costs.
[*] [b]Class config:[/b] new file to turn classes on or off.
[*] [b]Class config:[/b] disabled classes do not appear in the game, get no new points and give no bonuses.
