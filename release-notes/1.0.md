[h1]🌟 Update 1.0 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Official release with levels, XP, skill points, 27 classes, attributes, a new window with a Shop, extra chest slots, Explosive Miner and hotkeys.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Official release:[/b] RPG Skills has reached its official 1.0 release.
[*] [b]Official release:[/b] previous versions were tests, adjustments and learning steps until the mod got here.
[*] [b]Official release:[/b] the mod went through a big rework to be cleaner, clearer and ready to keep evolving.
[*] [b]Official release:[/b] the mod is no longer a set of tests and loose bonuses and becomes a complete RPG layer for Necesse.
[*] [b]Official release:[/b] the focus is to give your character more depth without removing the game's original style.
[*] [b]Progression:[/b] your character grows with levels, XP and skill points.
[*] [b]Progression:[/b] you earn XP by playing normally: defeating enemies, fighting bosses, mining, harvesting, fishing, planting, exploring and interacting with settlements.
[*] [b]Progression:[/b] when you level up, you gain points to invest in classes.
[*] [b]Progression:[/b] exploring, fighting, mining, fishing, building and taking care of the world feed a larger progression.
[*] [b]Progression:[/b] more reason to keep the same character and feel real growth across the world.
[*] [b]Classes:[/b] the class system was rebuilt with 27 classes.
[*] [b]Classes:[/b] the styles are melee, ranged, magic, summon, survival, defense, mobility, mining, fishing, loot, gold, building, trinkets and settlements.
[*] [b]Classes:[/b] each class strengthens a different part of the game, so you can build your character your way.
[*] [b]Classes:[/b] some classes unlock special effects such as vision, vein mining, elemental effects, healing, defense, mobility and utility bonuses.
[*] [b]Classes:[/b] Cavern Master unlocks Vein Miner to improve mining.
[*] [b]RPG Skills window:[/b] organized into three tabs: Status, Classes and Shop.
[*] [b]RPG Skills window:[/b] the Status tab shows level, XP, free points and general character info.
[*] [b]RPG Skills window:[/b] the Classes tab shows the classes and lets you spend skill points.
[*] [b]RPG Skills window:[/b] the Shop tab brings together temporary XP bonuses, world stack upgrades and extra chest slots.
[*] [b]RPG Skills window:[/b] the default shortcut to open and close it is K.
[*] [b]Shop:[/b] temporary XP bonuses speed up progression.
[*] [b]Shop:[/b] configurable hotkeys for the 2x, 5x and 10x XP bonuses.
[*] [b]Shop:[/b] world stack upgrade, which helps with storage and farming.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] the Shop sells global slot upgrades for the world's chests.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] each purchase adds +10 slots, starting at 5,000 coins, up to 400 slots.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] large chests get scrolling to keep slots accessible.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] the Shop has a reset that returns chests to the default 40 slots.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] before resetting, remove items from the extra slots.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] the mod tries to drop items from extra slots on the ground around the chest, but something may still be lost.
[*] [b]Chest slots:[/b] the reset does not refund coins.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] new Shop ability, bought per player.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] requires Cavern Master level 5 to buy and to use.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] if points are reset and the requirement drops, it stays blocked until the right level is reached again.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] turned on with its configurable hotkey, it makes any pickaxe break nearby blocks with an explosion effect.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] it does not hurt players or mobs and gives no XP for extra breaks.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] it only works with pickaxes and respects the power of the pickaxe used.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] it has limits to avoid freezes in very crowded areas.
[*] [b]Explosive Miner:[/b] it does not work together with Advanced Vein Miner mode.
[*] [b]Commands:[/b] /rpgskills set level <1-135> sets the player level and clears current experience.
[*] [b]Commands:[/b] /rpgskills maxskills sets the player to max level and maxes every class.
[*] [b]Commands:[/b] /rpgskills reset points refunds class points, charging the correct cost when needed.
[*] [b]Commands:[/b] /rpgskills reset zero fully resets progression and refunds reset coins.
