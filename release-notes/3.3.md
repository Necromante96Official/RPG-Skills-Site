[h1]🌟 Update 3.3 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Elemental Fusion, Inner Storm and the new Grimoire look for the skills window.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Elemental Fusion:[/b] with two damage-over-time masters at level 5, attacks start combining those effects.
[*] [b]Elemental Fusion:[/b] fire and frost shatter.
[*] [b]Elemental Fusion:[/b] poison and blood spread plague.
[*] [b]Elemental Fusion:[/b] fire and poison burn harder.
[*] [b]Elemental Fusion:[/b] blood and frost make the next hit heavier.
[*] [b]Elemental Fusion:[/b] nightmare with another effect can cause fear.
[*] [b]Inner Storm:[/b] with Bloodthirsty, Incendiary, Venomous, Freezing and Nightmare all at level 5, damage-over-time effects deal 50% more damage.
[/list]

[h2]🛠️ Quality of life[/h2]
[list]
[*] Each class shows its own item in the list and in the class detail, in the game's style.
[*] New Grimoire look for the skills window, with an opaque dark background and a gold frame.
[*] The skills window's tabs sit on the side.
[*] Each class level shows as gold pips.
[*] Text is easier to read.
[*] The world no longer shows through the window.
[/list]

[h2]🤝 Compatibility with Safe Haven QOL[/h2]
[list]
[*] With Safe Haven QOL 4.8, stackable vinyls now follow RPG Skills stack sizes.
[*] Options Safe Haven removed are no longer recreated in its config.
[/list]

[h2]🔧 Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*] Frozen Hemorrhage now really adds 30% to the next direct hit.
[*] Classes disabled in config no longer count toward Elemental Fusion or Inner Storm.
[*] A killing Shatter now credits the kill to the player.
[*] Each class's points (e.g. 0 / 5) are no longer cut off by the name button.
[/list]
