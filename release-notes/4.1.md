[h1]🌟 Update 4.1 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Icons above chests, a new XP panel and Status tab, station range in the Shop, a new inventory button, the chest filter on the side, more map revealed by classes, fairer Shop prices and fixes for the window shortcut and Necromancy names.

[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] when you get close, an icon shows up above the chest, so you know what is inside without opening it.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] two new buttons at the top of the chest window, next to the sorting ones, pick the icon and the style.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] click the button while holding an item to use that item.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] with an empty hand, the button switches between "automatic" and "none".
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] "automatic" shows the item there is most of in the chest and updates by itself.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] there are four styles, each with its own animation, and each chest picks its own.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] Bubble pops in with a little bounce.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] Wooden sign sits on the lid, grows and sways.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] RPG crest is purple and gold and gently pulses.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] Icon only leaves the item floating by itself, with no background or shadow.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] each icon fits within its own chest's width, so neighboring chests don't cover each other's icon.
[*] [b]Chest icon:[/b] the icon is saved in the chest and every player in the world sees it.
[*] [b]XP panel:[/b] the panel above the inventory now has a badge with your level.
[*] [b]XP panel:[/b] a bar fills with your XP, with the numbers and the percentage inside it.
[*] [b]XP panel:[/b] with the XP bonus bought in the Shop, the multiplier and the time left show up next to the bar.
[*] [b]Status tab:[/b] level, XP bar and points to spend sit in a single card, on top.

[*] [b]Status tab:[/b] instead of one huge list, bonuses show up in cards: Combat, Survival, Effects on enemies, Movement, Mysticism and summons, Gathering and utility.
[*] [b]Status tab:[/b] at the bottom are the abilities and passives you already have.
[*] [b]Status tab:[/b] also at the bottom is a summary of what the world bought in the Shop (stack, chest slots and station range).
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] new "Crafting Station Range" card in the Shop.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] with "use nearby storage" on, crafting stations and the upgrade station use chests farther away.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] the game uses 15 tiles, and each purchase adds 5 tiles, up to 50.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] the purchase belongs to the world, for every player, and is saved with it.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] the first level costs 2,000 coins, and each next one costs 2,000 more.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] when you join a server, the bought range only kicks in after the server sends it, so a station never shows a recipe you could not craft.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] cost, tiles per purchase and maximum range (up to 100) can be changed in the config panel.
[*] [b]Station range:[/b] administrators can put the range back to the game's, and coins are not refunded.
[*] [b]Inventory button:[/b] the button that opens RPG Skills swapped the mod logo for its own icon: two crossed swords with purple hilts and a golden star.
[*] [b]Inventory button:[/b] the button now has the same size and frame as the other buttons on the bar.
