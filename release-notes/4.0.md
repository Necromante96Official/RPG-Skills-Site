[h1]🌟 Atualização 4.0 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Pontos de classe mantidos nas atualizações, filtro do baú novo, Loja em cartões, vilas maiores, aliados da Necromancia com contorno, poção de vida sem espera e o mod mais leve.

[list]
[*] [b]Pontos nas atualizações:[/b] atualizar o RPG Skills não zera mais os pontos de classe.
[*] [b]Pontos nas atualizações:[/b] nesta e nas próximas versões, pontos gastos, nível, XP e compras da Loja continuam como estavam, sem precisar distribuir de novo.
[*] [b]Pontos nas atualizações:[/b] a opção "Respec de classes nas atualizações" saiu da configuração.
[*] [b]Filtro do baú:[/b] o filtro dos baús com slots extras usa as categorias do próprio jogo, as mesmas do filtro de armazenamento dos colonos.

[*] [b]Filtro do baú:[/b] nas subcategorias, um botão Voltar leva de volta para a categoria de cima.
[*] [b]Loja nova:[/b] a aba Loja foi refeita em cartões separados: Bônus de XP, Stack do mundo, Slots de Baús e Minerador Explosivo.

[*] [b]Loja nova:[/b] o cartão Stack do mundo explica que a compra aumenta quantos itens cabem em cada slot neste mundo, para todos os jogadores.
[*] [b]Loja nova:[/b] em 2x, um slot de 100 itens passa a guardar 200.
[*] [b]Loja nova:[/b] o cartão Slots de Baús diz que a compra adiciona slots aos baús do mundo para todos e quantos slots cada compra dá.
[*] [b]Loja nova:[/b] o buff de XP ganhou um ícone próprio, um orbe verde com seta para cima.
[*] [b]Expansão da vila:[/b] novo cartão na Loja que expande a vila onde você está, sem precisar abrir a bandeira.
[*] [b]Expansão da vila:[/b] o cartão mostra o tamanho atual, o próximo e o custo com os ícones dos itens.
[*] [b]Expansão da vila:[/b] só o dono da vila pode expandir.

[*] [b]Vilas maiores:[/b] cada nível extra pede moedas e um conjunto de materiais de fim de jogo que vai mudando (barras, gemas e essências).

[h1]🌟 Update 4.0 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Class points kept across updates, a new chest filter, a Shop in cards, bigger settlements, outlined Necromancy allies, health potions with no wait and a lighter mod.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Points across updates:[/b] updating RPG Skills no longer resets your class points.
[*] [b]Points across updates:[/b] in this and future versions, spent points, level, XP and Shop purchases stay as they were, with no need to spend them again.
[*] [b]Points across updates:[/b] the "Class respec on updates" option was removed from the config.
[*] [b]Chest filter:[/b] the filter for chests with extra slots uses the game's own categories, the same ones as the settlers' storage filter.
[*] [b]Chest filter:[/b] first you pick a group (Equipment, Materials, Consumable, Objects, Tiles...) and then go down into its subcategories, for example Equipment, Weapons and Melee weapons.
[*] [b]Chest filter:[/b] each group has its own colored icon, easy to recognize at a glance.
[*] [b]Chest filter:[/b] inside subcategories, a Back button takes you back to the category above.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] the Shop tab was rebuilt as separate cards: XP bonus, World stack, Chest slots and Explosive Miner.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] each card has an icon, an explanation of what the purchase does, the current state and its buttons.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] prices show the game's own item icons (coins, bars and dynamite) with the amount next to them.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] hover over an icon to see the item name.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] the World stack card explains that the purchase raises how many items fit in each slot in this world, for every player.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] at 2x, a 100-item slot holds 200.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] the Chest slots card says the purchase adds slots to the world's chests for everyone and how many slots each purchase gives.
[*] [b]New Shop:[/b] the XP buff has its own icon, a green orb with an up arrow.
[*] [b]Settlement expansion:[/b] a new Shop card that expands the settlement you are standing in, without opening the flag.
[*] [b]Settlement expansion:[/b] the card shows the current size, the next one and the cost with item icons.
[*] [b]Settlement expansion:[/b] only the settlement owner can expand it.
[*] [b]Settlement expansion:[/b] the card updates by itself when you walk into or out of a settlement with the Shop open.
[*] [b]Bigger settlements:[/b] after the game's 7 levels come 49 more, up to 1,840x1,840 tiles.
[*] [b]Bigger settlements:[/b] each extra level asks for coins and a rotating set of late-game materials (bars, gems and essences).
[*] [b]Bigger settlements:[/b] the amounts asked grow every level.
[*] [b]Bigger settlements:[/b] these levels also show up on the settlement flag.
[*] [b]Bigger settlements:[/b] very large settlements make the world heavier to run.
[*] [b]Ally outlines:[/b] allies summoned by the Necromancy ritual now have a glowing purple outline, easy to tell apart from enemies in the middle of a fight.
[*] [b]Ally outlines:[/b] Army of the Dead allies have a gold outline, so you know which ones will disappear.
[*] [b]Ally outlines:[/b] Army of the Dead is the 50% chance at level 5 that turns enemies into allies for 1 minute.
[*] [b]Health potion:[/b] by default, you and your settlers can drink health potions back to back, with no wait.
[*] [b]Health potion:[/b] the game's "Health potion fatigue" is no longer applied.
[*] [b]Health potion:[/b] in the config's Features tab, Potions section, you can change the wait for the player and for settlers.
[*] [b]Health potion:[/b] -1 goes back to the game value (30 seconds), and any other number becomes the wait in seconds.
[*] [b]Health potion:[/b] on a server, the server's config applies to every player.
[/list]

[h2]⚡ Performance[/h2]
[list]

[*] Settlers weigh less on the server in large colonies.
[*] The Machine Master, Nature Master and Trap Master bonuses cost the game less, even in very large settlements.
[*] Necromancy ritual allies weigh less while moving.
[*] With the Necromancy tab open during a fight, the RPG Skills window only updates what changed, instead of rebuilding everything several times per second.
[*] The on-screen XP bar costs less every frame.
[*] Players and monsters with buffs (speed, damage, defense) cost the game less.
[*] RPG Skills weighs less on the server for each connected player.

[list]

[*] Everything you change and save in the RPG Skills config stays saved when restarting the game, even after playing a world and going back to the menu.
[*] Monster names that do not fit on the card or in a Ritual slot scroll like a marquee, instead of being cut or overlapping the line below.
[*] Config symbols that showed up as "?" (like the Accelerated Sleep arrow and the dash in "0-255") were replaced in every language.
[*] Some config texts were fixed.
[*] On a server, RPG Skills chat notices (shop, mining, reset, Necromancy) arrive in your game's language, not the server's.
[*] Necromancy notices that did not show up in chat now do.
[*] When you join a server, RPG Skills uses its rules (XP, classes, shop costs, enabled options).
[*] Your config file does not change, and when you leave the server everything goes back to your own settings.
[*] Level-up fireworks, the inventory button and the status line stay the way you set them on the server.
[*] The Alchemy Master's potion duration bonus no longer stretches health and mana potion fatigue, which made alchemists wait longer to drink another one.
[*] That bonus now only applies to the good potion effects.
[*] The Alchemy potion duration bonus only applies to whoever drank it, without carrying over to the same potion for other players.
[*] If an RPG Skills bonus goes wrong, fishing and harvesting keep working normally.

[*] If the game closes in the middle of saving, the previous config stays intact instead of corrupted.
