[h1]🌟 Update 3.6 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Necromancy to master monsters and turn them into allies, a ritual bar with its own hotkeys and split team XP.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] defeat monsters to master them and turn them into allies that fight by your side.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] every monster you defeat counts one victory for that kind of monster.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] it works for everything that tries to kill you: common monsters of every biome, elites and bosses.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] with enough victories, the monster goes up a Tier, from 0/3 to 3/3.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] each Tier unlocks 1 ally of that monster.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] to summon, open the Grimoire, go to the Necromancy tab and click Invoke on the monster you want.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] the chat lets you know when a monster goes up a Tier.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] if an ally cannot be summoned, the chat explains why, like not enough victories or slots full.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] the ally follows you and attacks enemies like a summon.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] the ally is immortal and enemies ignore it.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] everything translated into the mod's 12 languages.
[*] [b]Necromancy:[/b] for admins, /rpgskills necro setkills <monster> <victories> sets a monster's victories, handy for testing.
[*] [b]Victories:[/b] common monsters need 150 / 300 / 500 victories.
[*] [b]Victories:[/b] elite monsters, the stronger ones, need 300 / 600 / 1000 victories.
[*] [b]Victories:[/b] bosses need 50 / 100 / 150 victories.
[*] [b]Victories:[/b] bosses already count victories but can't be summoned yet, and will arrive as miniatures in a future update.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] you can have up to 3 ritual allies at the same time.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] they don't use your regular summon slots, and you can use both together.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] they stay with you until you remove them.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] they come back on their own when you join the world again.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] they deal the real damage of that monster.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] they move at the same speed as the game's summons and walk through doors like them.
[*] [b]Ritual allies:[/b] with all 3 allies of the same monster and that monster at Tier 3/3, you deal double damage (+100%) against that kind of monster.
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] the Ritual bar, on the right side of the screen, shows your 3 allies.
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] a highlighted slot is a summoned ally, and a normal slot is a resting ally.
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] each slot has its own key, picked in Settings > Controls (look for "Ritual ally").
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] you can also click the slot.
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] pressing the key calls the ally: you cast a purple and green magic circle and it bursts into the world in a flash of energy.
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] pressing it again puts the ally away, and it fades into a dark mist.
[*] [b]Ritual bar:[/b] other players see the effects too.
[*] [b]Necromancy tab:[/b] monsters show up as cards with icon, victories and progress.
[*] [b]Necromancy tab:[/b] cards are grouped by biome, in the order you meet them in the game (Forest, Spider nest, Vampire crypt, Plains, Snow, Swamp, Desert, Dungeon, Temple...).
[*] [b]Necromancy tab:[/b] quick filters All, Common, Elite and Boss.
[/list]

[h2]⚠️ Important[/h2]
[list]
[*] Your saves keep working.
[*] Necromancy starts from zero and none of your current progress changes.
[*] Allies are never saved in the world, so removing the mod leaves no monsters behind.
[/list]

[h2]🔧 Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*] When playing with friends on the same team, XP is now split between you.
[*] Before, each player got the full amount, so playing as a pair gave double the XP of playing solo.
[*] Opening Settings > Controls no longer floods the game's error log.
[*] Mod hotkeys without a key now show as "NOT SET", like the game's.
[/list]
