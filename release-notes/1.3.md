[h1]🌟 Update 1.3 - RPG Skills 🌟[/h1]

Trap Master, Machine Master and Nature Master, Efficient Administration and category filters in expanded chests.

[h2]✨ What's new[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Trap Master:[/b] new 5-level class.
[*] [b]Trap Master:[/b] increases trap damage inside settlements.
[*] [b]Trap Master:[/b] reduces trap damage outside settlements for players and colonists.
[*] [b]Trap Master:[/b] blocks trap damage to players and colonists inside settlements.
[*] [b]Trap Master:[/b] at level 5, settlement traps apply bleeding to enemies they hit.
[*] [b]Machine Master:[/b] new 5-level class.
[*] [b]Machine Master:[/b] speeds up all machines.
[*] [b]Machine Master:[/b] fuel lasts longer in fueled machines.
[*] [b]Machine Master:[/b] each level has fixed times for forge, cheese press, compost bin and grain mill.
[*] [b]Machine Master:[/b] at level 5, forge, cheese press, compost bin and grain mill process in 2 seconds.
[*] [b]Machine Master:[/b] logs in the forge last 80s, 120s, 240s, 360s and 600s, by level.
[*] [b]Nature Master:[/b] new 5-level class.
[*] [b]Nature Master:[/b] speeds up crops, trees, fruit trees, berry bushes and mushrooms in player or team settlements.
[*] [b]Nature Master:[/b] growth becomes 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 300% faster, by level.
[*] [b]Efficient Administration:[/b] new Realm Master passive at level 5.
[*] [b]Efficient Administration:[/b] colonists work faster.
[*] [b]Efficient Administration:[/b] expeditions take less time.
[*] [b]Chest filters:[/b] expanded chests have a fixed category filter panel beside the grid.
[*] [b]Chest filters:[/b] the panel helps find consumables, materials, equipment and other items.
[*] [b]Commands:[/b] administrators directly adjust the chest slot limit and world stack.
[/list]

[h2]⚡ Performance[/h2]
[list]
[*] Sorting large chests is lighter, with fewer stutters from the sort button or shortcut.
[/list]

[h2]🤝 Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] With Safe Haven QOL on, seed, sapling and fruit tree tooltips show growth time with the Nature Master bonus.
[*] RPG Skills fixes the pt-BR translation of Necesse 1.2.0 texts that were still in English.
[/list]

[h2]🔧 Fixes[/h2]
[list]
[*] The maxskills command correctly updates the player's bonuses, including on the stats screen.
[*] Players with max health and max mana bonuses enter the world with them full, if they were full before.
[*] The game's trinket slot upgrade items work alongside the Relic Master bonus.
[/list]
